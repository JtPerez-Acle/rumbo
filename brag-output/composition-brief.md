# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Aprende IA

## Objective
Create a short launch-style brag video for Aprende IA — a Spanish-language learning
platform that turns "what you want to be" into a verified study route and a work
document under the learner's byline.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape — 1920x1080
- Duration: 20 seconds

## Source Material
- Project root: `C:\Users\joset\Projects\MoneyPrinterTurbo`
- Primary files read: `studio/dashboard/static/learn.html` (the learner SPA and its
  design-token layer), `studio/dashboard/static/ruta.html` (the public route page),
  `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/01`, `docs/09`
- Product name: **Aprende IA**
- Tagline / strongest claim: *"Dinos qué quieres ser. Nosotros armamos el plan."*
- Key UI moment to recreate: the route rendered as ordered capability steps, then the

- Copy that must appear verbatim (all real strings from the product):
  - `Dinos qué quieres ser.`
  - `Paso 1 de 13`
  - `Sabrás pedirle a la IA exactamente lo que necesitas`

  - `Lo publicamos a propósito.`

## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: **polished**
- Creative direction: *quiet night-workshop product film*
- Interpretation: slow confident cuts, generous holds on the two lines that carry the
  argument, motion that reveals rather than performs. A video about honesty must not
  look like an ad.
- Angle: the platform that publishes what it *cannot* teach you. The coverage number
  is computed so a gap always costs coverage, and below a floor it refuses to promise
  a deliverable at all. The honesty is the product.
- Hook: one amber lamp resolving out of black under the line *Dinos qué quieres ser.*
- Outro / punchline: *No un certificado. Tu trabajo.*
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS language
  - Abstract filler visuals
  - Any claim of users, traction, or social proof (this is a 5-learner alpha)
  - Unrelated visual redesign — the palette is the product's own

## Storyboard
Creative contract is `brag-output/brag-plan.md`.

Scene summary:
1. **Hook** — 3.2s — black; amber lamp resolves; *Dinos qué quieres ser.*
2. **Route** — 6.3s — posting collapses into ordered capability steps (beat-timed at
   ~4.6 / 5.6 / 6.6s); coverage meter fills to 83%
3. **The gap** — 5.0s — *Esto lo pide el puesto y no lo cubrimos* + three real gaps;
   held beat, then *Lo publicamos a propósito.*
4. **Work becomes paper** — 4.0s — submission and three rubric dimensions in the dark
   interface, then the paper-white document with byline at the 17.0s strong cue
5. **Outro** — 1.5s — *No un certificado. Tu trabajo.* + wordmark; lamp dims

## Audio
- Audio role: warm restrained bed with sparse motion-matched accents
- Audio arc: near-silence under the hook → bed enters ~3.0s → softens under the gap
  admission so the line reads quietly → gentle fade from ~18.5s
- Music: `assets/audio/music.mp3` (happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1, 120.19 BPM)
- Cues: strong beats at 16.02 / 17.02 / 18.02 / 18.52 / 20.02s; beat grid ~0.5s from
  3.02s. Paper-turn on the 17.02s cue; route steps on the grid between 4.5–7.0s.
- Audio-reactive: subtle — lamp glow breathes with RMS. No waveform/equalizer visuals.
- SFX: one soft click on the posting collapse; one restrained drop on the paper turn.
- Restraint rule: no riser before the gap admission. It is stated, not sold.
